Mirai School of Technology

(Hi-Tech Institute of Engineering & Technology)

Mirai School of Technology operates in Ghaziabad, through its partner campus, the Hi-Tech Institute of Engineering & Technology, which is AICTE approved and affiliated with AKTU. The campus offers B.Tech programs in Computer Science Engineering (AI/ML). The educational model emphasizes hands-on coding and product building from the first year, teaching core skills in Python, JavaScript, React, and AI/ML basics to help students build a working portfolio early in their academic journey.

About

Mirai School of Technology is a sophisticated and modern institute that focuses on project-based, hands-on learning that mimics real-world challenges. From the beginning itself, students don't just sit for scheduled lectures and classes; they build revolutionary apps to solve user-centric problems, writing code to experiment, and analyzing data for valuable insights.

When students build websites or create AI tools, they pick up skills by doing the work. The labs buzz with energy and new ideas more like places where innovation happens. Mirai School of Technology doesn't wait to give students hands-on experience like many colleges do. Instead, it gets them working on actual projects right away. This helps students learn by doing, not just by memorizing principles, syntax and theories.

MIRAI's mission is simple: to move beyond theory and create engineers who can design, deploy, and lead in the AI-powered world. This is not traditional engineering. This is engineering, rebuilt for the AI era.

At Mirai, students don't just study AI. They Build with it.

Features:

  • Campus and infrastructure are engineered for creators. Smart classrooms, advanced computing labs, robotics facilities, and Apple-powered development environments give students the tools to experiment, prototype, and ship, not just attend lectures.
  • MIRAI is led by technologists, not just academics. Students learn directly from engineers, founders, and practitioners who are actively building with AI, ensuring what is taught in the classroom stays aligned with what is demanded by the industry.
  • Admission is selective. Only the top 12% of applicants are chosen, creating a focused, high-performing peer group that drives ambition, collaboration, and excellence.
